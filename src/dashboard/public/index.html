Outbound Peers

Letting Bitcoin Core connect out over Tor and I2P as well as clearnet spreads your node's paths further.

Why

Slot Fill adds one Tor and one I2P peer to the manual peers, and Core needs those networks switched on to reach them. Tor and I2P peers are seldom first; their paths are slower, and that is expected.

Slot Fill

Fills free manual slots and spreads them: one per region, one per provider, one Tor and one I2P peer.

How it works
  • When a manual slot is free, it goes to a peer from a missing region, taken from Core's address book.
  • Core tests it. If Core can't connect, the next one is tried.
  • A manual peer that is offline for an hour frees its slot. One that ever delivered a block first gets a day.
  • Working peers and Core's own connections are never touched.
