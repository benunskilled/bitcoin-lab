Peer Rotation

Every ~10 minutes, at most one promotion per pass:

  • Makes the best peer that really delivers manual - into a free slot, or in place of a manual peer it clearly beats.
  • Drops an outbound peer that has been connected through 50 blocks without ever delivering one first. Core replaces it with a new random one.
  • Frees the slot of a manual peer that has gone offline. The better its record, the longer it keeps the slot first.

A new slot holder is safe for its first 50 blocks. A peer that loses a slot is parked below, re-tested, and back as soon as it answers.
