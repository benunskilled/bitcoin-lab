Peer Rotation

Every ~10 minutes, at most one promotion per pass:

  • Promotes the best deliverer to manual, into a free slot or in place of a weaker one.
  • Drops an outbound peer that was connected for 50 of the last 500 blocks without delivering one first; Core picks a new one.
  • Frees the slot of a manual peer that went offline. The better its record, the longer it may stay away.

New slot holders are safe for 50 blocks. Peers that lose their slot while offline are parked below and come back when they answer.
