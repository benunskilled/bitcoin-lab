Peer Rotation
Every ~10 minutes: drops outbound peers that have had a full day to prove themselves and never delivered a block first - Core replaces each with a fresh, random one - and reclaims the slot of any manual peer that has been offline longer than its own First % has earned it (a few hours at 1%, a week at 40%). Those peers are parked, not forgotten: they are re-tested until they answer and then take their slot straight back. How hard they are chased scales the same way - a peer worth 30% is knocked on twice a day for months, one worth 1% every other day for a few days. Whatever slot is then free goes to the best candidate available. One peer joins the manual set per pass.
